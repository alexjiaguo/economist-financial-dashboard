### **Step 1: Select Asset Type**
Choose from:
- **Currency Pair**: Trade between any two currencies
- **Stock**: Monitor individual company stocks
- **Cryptocurrency**: Track crypto prices and signals
- **Index**: Follow market indices and ETFs

### **Step 2: Choose Your Assets**

### **Step 4: Monitor & Trade**
